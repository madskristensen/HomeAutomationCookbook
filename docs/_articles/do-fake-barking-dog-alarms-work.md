# Do fake barking dog alarms work?

{% include content-hero.html src="/assets/img/social/guides/do-fake-barking-dog-alarms-work.svg" alt="A motion sensor near a door triggers a speaker that plays a barking dog sound" %}

A fake barking dog alarm can make someone pause. It cannot stop a break-in. Treat it as one extra layer on top of locks, lights, and a real alarm. It works best when it plays only while you are away, sounds like it comes from inside, and does not bark at every passerby. The [dog-bark intrusion deterrent recipe](/automation/security/fake-dog-deterrent.html) shows how to build one with a motion sensor and a speaker you may already have.

## The short answer

| Question | Honest answer |
|---|---|
| Will it fool everyone? | No. It does not have to. A short pause can be enough to change an opportunistic plan. |
| Is it a security system? | No. It does not call anyone or record anything. |
| When does it help most? | When the house is empty and the sound starts as someone gets close. |
| What ruins it? | Barking at delivery drivers, neighbors, or your own family. |

## Two ways to get one

### A standalone barking dog alarm

These are single devices with a built-in motion sensor and speaker. They are simple to set up. Check that you can change the volume and sensitivity, and turn it off easily when you are home. Read reviews for false triggers and battery life before you buy.

### Build it with your smart home

Use a motion sensor or camera you already own, plus a smart speaker. The rule plays a barking clip only in Away mode, with a cooldown between plays. You control exactly when it runs, and it fits your other away routines. This is the approach in the [recipe](/automation/security/fake-dog-deterrent.html).

## What makes it believable

- **Sound from inside:** a speaker near the front door, not outside.
- **Short and varied:** a few barks, then quiet. Several clips help.
- **Starts fast:** the sound should follow the motion within a second or two.
- **Pairs with lights:** add [away lights](/automation/security/away-lights.html) so the house also looks lived in.

## What to avoid

- **Running while people are home.** Require Away mode, not just a schedule.
- **Sensors that see the street.** Aim at the doorstep or path, not the sidewalk. The [motion sensor placement guide](/articles/motion-sensor-placement.html) helps.
- **Endless barking.** Use a cooldown, so the neighbors do not learn to ignore it.

## Failure modes

- **Plays too often:** Narrow the sensor's view, or lengthen the cooldown.
- **No sound:** Check that the speaker is online and test the clip by hand.
- **Plays when you come home:** Make sure arriving turns off Away mode before the door opens.

## FAQ

### Do burglars believe fake dog barking?

Some may, some will not. The goal is a moment of hesitation and a sign that the house reacted, not a perfect fake.

### Is a barking dog alarm better than a camera?

They do different jobs. A camera records and alerts you. A barking alarm makes noise on site. Many homes use both.

### Will a fake dog alarm bother my neighbors?

It can if it is loud or frequent. Keep the volume moderate, run it only when you are away, and add a cooldown.
