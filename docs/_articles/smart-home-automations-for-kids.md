# Smart home automations for kids and family routines

Kids do better with clear cues than with a parent repeating the same thing. These recipes let the house give those cues. A speaker calls out the next step. A plug notices the toothbrush went back to charge. Parents step in only when something did not happen.

Each one works with common gear and keeps a manual way to skip or override it.

## School mornings

- [School morning countdown on any smart speaker](/automation/notifications/school-morning-countdown.html). A speaker calls out brush teeth and shoes at set times on school days.
- [Spoken school morning reminders with Sonos](/automation/notifications/school-morning-reminders.html). The same idea on a Sonos speaker, and the music picks back up after each reminder.

## Bedtime

- [Bedtime countdown for kids on any smart speaker](/automation/notifications/bedtime-countdown.html). Timed steps from last call for snacks to brushing teeth.
- [Check that kids brushed their teeth at bedtime](/automation/notifications/teeth-brushing-reminder.html). A smart plug sees the toothbrush start charging, and a reminder plays only if it never did.

## Babies and naps

- [Set a nursery quiet mode](/automation/notifications/baby-sleep-mode.html). A caregiver turns on quiet mode for naps, and urgent alerts still get through.

## Where to start

Start with the countdown for the time of day that feels most rushed. It needs only a speaker you may already own. Add the toothbrushing check once the bedtime countdown runs smoothly.
