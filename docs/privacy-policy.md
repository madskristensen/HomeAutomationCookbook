# Privacy policy

**Last updated:** September 12, 2026

Home Automation Cookbook is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how the site handles visitor data.

## Information we collect

### Microsoft Clarity analytics

Home Automation Cookbook uses Microsoft Clarity to understand how visitors use the site and to improve its content, navigation, accessibility, and performance. Clarity captures website usage through behavioral metrics, heatmaps, and session replay. This may include pages visited, interactions with page elements, referral information, and browser or device information.

Clarity uses cookies and other tracking technologies to collect this usage data. The information is used for site optimization and troubleshooting, not for advertising on Home Automation Cookbook.

Microsoft processes Clarity data under its own privacy terms. Read the [Microsoft Privacy Statement](https://www.microsoft.com/privacy/privacystatement) and [Microsoft Clarity privacy information](https://learn.microsoft.com/clarity/setup-and-installation/privacy-disclosure) for more information about collection, use, protection, and retention.

You can limit cookies through your browser settings. Blocking Clarity or its cookies does not prevent you from reading the site.

### Information you provide

Home Automation Cookbook does not require account registration or maintain a customer or mailing-list database. If you contact the project or contribute through GitHub, the information you choose to provide is handled by GitHub under its privacy policy.

Home Automation Cookbook does not:

- Require account registration
- Operate an email newsletter
- Use visitor analytics for advertising
- Sell or share data with data brokers

## GitHub contributions

If you contribute to this project on GitHub:

- Your GitHub username and contributions are publicly visible
- GitHub's privacy policy applies to your use of their platform
- You can review GitHub's privacy policy at [github.com/privacy](https://github.com/site/privacy)

## Third-party links

Our site contains links to external resources and products. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these external sites. We encourage you to read their privacy policies.

## External services mentioned

Some guides discuss smart-home platforms and link to product pages, communities, GitHub, Microsoft Clarity documentation, and other external services. Their privacy policies apply when you visit or use them.

## Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue on our [GitHub repository](https://github.com/madskristensen/HomeAutomationCookbook/issues).

## Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
