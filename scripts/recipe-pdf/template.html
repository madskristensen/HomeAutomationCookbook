How to use these cards

Each card is a short version of a full web recipe. Recipes are numbered, and the contents and index point to those numbers.

When is the trigger. Only if lists the conditions. Do is the action. Stop is what ends it.

Scan the QR code, or type the short link, for the full recipe with setup notes, failure modes, and device options.

Devices are examples of what fits the job. Check compatibility with your own hub before you buy.

Product names are examples, not endorsements of fitness for your home. Test every rule before you rely on it. Safety devices such as smoke, CO, and leak alarms must keep working without automation.
